Job Opportunities

We advertise job opportunities related to the intersection of network science and artificial intelligence. If you have a position to advertise, please contact us at kalokagathos.agon@gmail.com, mac.w.obrien@gmail.com or ruben.christon@gmail.com.

Postdoc in Brain-Inspired Sparse AI and Complex Systems Intelligence - Shuimu Scholar

Institution:Center for Complex Network Intelligence (CCNI), Tsinghua Laboratory of Brain and Intelligence (THBI), Tsinghua University

Location: Beijing, China

Position Type: Postdoctoral Fellow (2-3 years)

Requirements:

  • Under 35 years old and completed their Ph.D. within the past three years.
  • Ph.D. in computer science, physics, applied mathematics or a related field.
  • Published high-level papers in related fields such as complex networks, machine learning, and theoretical computer science (top conferences/journals).
  • Outstanding mathematical and programming skills (such as Python, MATLAB).
  • Excellent written and oral communication skills in English.
  • Capable of collaborating in an international team.
  • Candidates specialized in AI, robotics, or Data Science are eligible for additional funding via the Huiyan program (a supplementary add-on to the Shuimu Fellowship).

Description: Research focus on brain-inspired artificial intelligence, complex systems and network science, efficient large-scale model training, and network topology and geometry.

How to Apply: Send email to kalokagathos.agon@gmail.com and ccni.thbi@gmail.com with a CV, 2-3 representative papers, a list of publications and Google Scholar links, information of recommenders (optional).

More Information: Shuimu Tsinghua Scholar Program
